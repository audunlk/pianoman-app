pianoman-app

A small personal tool that uploads its developer's own piano music videos to the developer's own YouTube channel, through the YouTube Data API (YouTube API Services).

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video file, chosen by its owner, to the YouTube channel the owner signed in with, as a private or scheduled video.
  • Sets that video's title, description, tags, thumbnail, translations and publishing time, and adds it to one of the channel's own playlists.

Who it is for

It runs on its developer's own computer for one YouTube channel. It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, no hosted app and no server. Nobody else's account is used.

The permission it asks for

One Google permission, "Manage your YouTube account" (scope youtube.force-ssl), used only for the steps above. See the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed and stored, and how to revoke access.
