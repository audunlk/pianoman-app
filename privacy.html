pianoman-app

Privacy policy

Effective 6 October 2026.

pianoman-app is a personal tool, made by the GitHub user audunlk ("the developer"), that uploads the developer's own videos to the developer's own YouTube channel.

YouTube API Services

pianoman-app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data that Google processes when the app calls the YouTube API is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

When you authorize pianoman-app, Google gives it access to your YouTube account with the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl. The app uses that access only to:

  • upload the video files you choose, and set their title, description, tags, thumbnail, translations, privacy setting and publishing time;
  • read your channel's name, your playlists' titles and YouTube's list of video categories, to put each video in the right playlist and category, and create that playlist if it does not exist.

It does not read your viewing history, comments, subscribers, messages, analytics or any other information.

What is stored, and where

  • The authorization (an OAuth refresh token) is stored only on the computer that runs the app, in Windows Credential Manager.
  • The ID and link of each uploaded video are written to a file next to that video, on the same computer.

Nothing is stored anywhere else. The developer runs no server, and receives none of your data.

Sharing and use

The app sends data only to Google, through the YouTube API, to do the steps above. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any model.

Cookies and tracking

The app uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. This website has none either. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Revoke the app's access at any time in your Google Account's security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Delete the stored authorization by removing the classical-piano-youtube entry in Windows Credential Manager.
  • Videos you uploaded stay on your channel until you delete them in YouTube Studio.

Children

pianoman-app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and its effective date changed.
